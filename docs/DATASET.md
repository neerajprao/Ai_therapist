## Speakers

Five-class clips per speaker range from 36 to 1000. The largest: {'tess_OAF': 1000, 'tess_YAF': 1000, 'savee_in_Angry': 90, 'savee_in_Happy': 90, 'savee_in_Neutral': 90, 'savee_in_Sad': 90}.

Speaker IDs come from filenames: the actor number for RAVDESS and CREMA-D, and the `OAF`/`YAF` prefix for TESS. SAVEE's speaker prefix was removed upstream; each of four class folders holds one complete 120-clip set of distinct audio, so the folder name is used as the speaker key. That is an inference, not ground truth.

## Label problems found

- **SAVEE is filed under the wrong folders.** 405 of 480 SAVEE clips sit in a folder whose emotion differs from the emotion code in the filename. The filename is used.
- **Exact duplicate audio:** 4 groups (8 files), 3 of them with two different labels for identical audio. Examples: [['Angry/1017_IWW_ANG_XX.wav', 'Fearful/1017_IWW_FEA_XX.wav'], ['Disgusted/1013_WSI_DIS_XX.wav', 'Sad/1013_WSI_SAD_XX.wav'], ['Happy/03-01-03-01-02-01-07.wav', 'Happy/03-01-03-01-02-02-07.wav'], ['Happy/1006_TIE_HAP_XX.wav', 'Neutral/1006_TIE_NEU_XX.wav']].
- Two RAVDESS filename variants (`103-...`) and one TESS typo (`OA_bite_neutral.wav`) are parsed as their intended forms.

## Clip length and sample rate

Five-class clips: min 1.254 s, median 2.469 s, 95th percentile 4.538 s, max 7.139 s. 27.6% are longer than the 3 s training window.

Sample rates: {'48000': 2076, '16000': 7442, '24414': 2799, '44100': 480, '96000': 1}. Everything is resampled to 16 kHz.
